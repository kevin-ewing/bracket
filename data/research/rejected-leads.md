# Additional research decisions

These leads were examined during the edition 4 review but are not active questions. They are not included in the numeric historical screening count.

- **All shirts produced in Vietnam:** the NSO yearbook landing page did not expose a verifiable national shirt-count table. Garments, apparel exports and shirts are not interchangeable. Use a scoped Adidas footwear/Vietnam question with an explicit manufacturer report instead. Do not invent a national shirt total.
- **Statue of Liberty copper mass:** NPS pages and secondary accounts give conflicting masses. Excluded pending a reconciled definition/source. [NPS facts](https://www.nps.gov/stli/learn/statue-of-liberty-facts.htm).
- **Ellis Island throughput:** NPS pages describe differing operating windows with rounded totals. The period matters; excluded instead of treating the different windows as equivalent. [NPS immigration](https://www.nps.gov/elis/learn/historyculture/places_immigration.htm).
- **Airbnb nights and experiences:** mixes different units into a proprietary KPI. Not a clean everyday target. [Airbnb results](https://news.airbnb.com/airbnb-q4-2023-and-full-year-financial-results).
- **Apple App Store economic activity:** ecosystem sales are not app purchases; the large estimate requires a long scope explanation. Excluded. [Apple release](https://www.apple.com/uk/newsroom/2023/05/one-point-one-trillion-generated-in-app-store-ecosystem-in-2022/).
- **Global milk, cocoa and coffee output:** search results mixed harvest years, forecasts and later revisions. Not added without a clearly frozen and reconciled table.
- **US coin output:** calendar production and fiscal shipments differ; conflicting totals appeared in secondary summaries. Not added.
- **BIC daily product sales:** combines pens, lighters and razors, weakening the mental model. Use a specifically scoped pen-writing-distance benchmark instead.
- **Blue whale heart mass:** conflicting specimen masses and vague “typical” claims. Excluded rather than averaging them.
- **Human cell count:** reference-body definitions and cell categories vary; scientifically interesting but unnecessarily technical for this release.
- **Sun mass loss:** radiation and solar-wind loss are distinct; an unspecified mass-loss prompt would be misleading. Excluded.
- **Ocean oxygen share:** gross production, atmospheric inventory and net addition are easily confused. Excluded rather than using a vague percentage question.
- **Library/museum collection totals:** accession categories mix specimens, volumes, manuscripts and archival units. No convincing everyday route to a tightly scoped count was established.
- **LEGO output:** no verified annual brick/unit count obtained from the official financial report; secondary totals were not imported.

Retained observed questions use original prompts and estimation routes. The source benchmark is not inferred from the illustrative route.
